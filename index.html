Testing & Commissioning Protocols

Methodical execution adhering to international IS / IEC standards

01

Pre-Test & Visual Inspection

Comprehensive checks of physical mounting, internal clearance, IP rating integrity, component labeling, and schematic verification prior to energization.

02

Torque & Mechanical Verification

IS/IEC Compliant

Calibrated torque wrench verification on all busbar joints, power cable terminations, and structural fasteners to eliminate loose contact thermal hotspots.

03

Continuity & Ductor Testing

Low-resistance contact resistance (micro-ohm) measurements across circuit breaker poles, busbar joints, and earth bonds to guarantee minimal voltage drop.

04

HiPot & Insulation Resistance (IR)

Pre-test and post-test dielectric withstand voltage testing (HiPot) and multi-kV insulation resistance (Megger) between phases and phase-to-earth.

05

Secondary Injection & Protection

Testing of numerical and static protection relays (overcurrent, earth fault, short circuit, under/over-voltage) and validation of tripping curves.

06

Logic & Breaker Operations

Verification of electrical/mechanical interlocks, auto-transfer logic (ATS/AMF), breaker open/close timing, and emergency shut-off mechanisms.
